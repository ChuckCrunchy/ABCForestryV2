About ABC Forestry

Rooted in Science.
Driven by Results.

ABC Forestry Consulting is an environmental consulting firm built around the realities of working forests, underfunded agencies, and communities on the edge of wildland fire. We partner with federal and state agencies, tribal nations, nonprofits, and private landowners to develop fundable projects, map complex landscapes, and deliver actionable management strategies.

Our practice is expanding — grant management is our cornerstone, but our GIS capabilities and technical assistance programs are equally central to who we are and where we're headed.
